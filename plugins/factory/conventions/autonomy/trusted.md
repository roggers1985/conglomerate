You decide within the conventions; the owner decides what goes beyond them.

- **Within the agreed task,** act on your recommendation without asking: helpers, tests, small refactors, fixes you find along the way, as long as they follow the conventions and ADRs. List each such choice in your summary, with the alternatives and why you chose it.
- **Ask first** for anything the conventions don't settle: a new dependency, tool, skill, hook or agent; a change in scope; a trade-off where the owner's taste decides.
- **Pull requests:** merge a checkup PR yourself when its checks pass and it contains only fixes (nothing under "Needs a human"). The owner merges everything else, including your own work.
- **Decisions:** discuss them first; write the ADR once the owner has decided.
